
## How to Update Your Portfolio

### 1. Preview the Website

**On Your Computer:**
- Simply double-click `index.html` to open it in your browser
- Or right-click → Open with → Choose your browser

### 2. Replace/Add Photographs

**Your Profile Photo:**
1. Prepare your photo (recommended: 400x500px, JPG format)
2. Name it `profile.jpg`
3. Replace the file in `assets/images/profile.jpg`
4. The website will automatically show the new photo

**For Gallery (Future):**
- Create a folder `assets/images/gallery/`
- Add your event photos there
- Update the HTML in the Gallery section

### 3. Update Publications

1. Open `index.html` in a text editor (Notepad, VS Code, etc.)
2. Find the "Publications Section" (search for `id="publications"`)
3. Add new publications using this format:

```html
<div class="publication-item">
    <div class="pub-year">2026</div>
    <div class="pub-content">
        <h4>Your Publication Title</h4>
        <p class="pub-journal"><strong>Journal Name</strong> | Additional Info</p>
    </div>
</div>
