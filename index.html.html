About Me

I am an Assistant Professor of English at Vignan's Foundation for Science, Technology & Research (VFSTR), where I serve as the designated AI Champion for the Department of English and Other Indian & Foreign Languages.

With over 9 years of teaching experience, I specialize in the intersection of English literature, communication skills, and artificial intelligence. My academic journey has been driven by a passion for exploring how emerging technologies can enhance traditional humanities education while maintaining the depth and richness of literary scholarship.

My research focuses on Diaspora Literature and Gender Studies, with particular emphasis on the works of Taslima Nasrin. I completed my Ph.D. at Annamalai University in 2025, examining wilderness and incredulity ideologies in women across select fictional works.

As an educator, I am committed to AI-integrated teaching methodologies, prompt engineering for academic excellence, and developing employability skills for literature students. I actively organize international conferences, national seminars, and workshops, and serve as a resource person for institutions across India.
